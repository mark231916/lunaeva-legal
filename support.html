Support

Short answers for the things most people ask first.

Where is my data stored?

By default, Lunaeva stores your record locally on your iPhone. If you enable Apple Health, related menstrual-flow samples are exchanged with Apple Health on your device.

How do I export my data?

Open Settings → Your data → Export raw CSV. The CSV export is available to every user, including without a Pro subscription.

Why is my prediction paused?

Predictions pause when hormonal contraception or pregnancy is selected because a natural-cycle estimate would not be reliable. You can update this context in Settings.

Why is the estimate only a range?

Cycle history varies. Lunaeva widens the window and lowers confidence when data is limited, cycles are irregular, or recent circumstances may affect timing.

How do I manage my subscription?

Open Settings in Lunaeva and choose Manage subscription, or use your Apple ID subscription settings. Existing purchases can be restored from the Pro screen.

Contact

Report a non-sensitive support issue at the support tracker. Do not post cycle records, notes, or other health information publicly.

Lunaeva is not an emergency or medical-advice service. Contact local emergency services or a qualified clinician for urgent concerns.
