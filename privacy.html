Lunaeva

Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Lunaeva is designed to keep sensitive cycle and symptom information under your control.

Information stored by Lunaeva

Cycle dates, flow, symptoms, moods, notes, optional TTC observations, and app preferences are stored in the app's private database on your iPhone. We do not operate a Lunaeva account service or backend and do not receive this information.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Lunaeva imports recent menstrual-flow samples and can save new period-flow records. Health access is optional. Lunaeva does not use Health data for advertising and does not transmit it to third parties. You can revoke access in the Health app or iOS Settings. Deleting Lunaeva data does not automatically delete records stored in Apple Health.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through your Apple ID. Lunaeva receives entitlement status, not your payment-card details. Apple's privacy policy applies to App Store purchases.

Tracking and analytics

Lunaeva does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. App Store crash diagnostics may be provided by Apple only if you have enabled sharing in iOS settings.

Export and deletion

You can export a raw CSV from Settings at any time. You can delete all Lunaeva data from Settings. Removing the app also removes its local database, subject to iOS backup behavior. Apple Health records must be managed separately in Apple Health.

Children

Lunaeva is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Medical limits

Lunaeva provides educational estimates. It does not diagnose a condition, confirm ovulation, or replace a qualified healthcare professional. Never use Lunaeva as contraception.

Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the Lunaeva support tracker. Do not include health records or other sensitive personal information in a public support request.

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change and will revise the effective date above.
